      Export parts

      Each ticked item becomes its own file: footage as picture, sound or both; sound as WAV or MP3; titles and captions as alpha renders; and the words of that range, re-based to zero so they line up with the sound. Run the sound through a voice tool, then Replace sound with… brings it back.

      Extract from footage

      Frames and sprite sheets land in Assets, where any clip can use them. A sprite sheet plays real footage inside an animation clip. Sub-clips land in Media.

      Export captions

      One subtitle file, timed as the captions fall on the timeline — so it lines up with the rendered video. Plain text gives the words alone, one line per caption.

      Snippets

      Replaces the current clip's HTML, CSS and JS.
